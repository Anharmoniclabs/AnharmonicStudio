ANHARMONIC STUDIO - DESIGN NOTES

The existing A-wave-dot identity was refined using the built-in image generation tool.
The clean black-on-white reference is included for provenance. It is not a transparent asset.
Production symbols were converted to real Bezier vector paths. Logo typography was
rebuilt with URW Gothic Book and converted to outlines; logo files require no fonts.
The primary black/white identity is complemented by an optional #427BFF blue accent.

Generation prompt (final selected source):
Create the production symbol for this exact existing brand. Keep the A, its lower sine
wave crossing through the A, and its separate small round dot on the right. Remove
all text including ANHARMONIC and STUDIO. Reproduce the entire A-wave-dot silhouette
as perfectly flat solid black on a uniform white background. Preserve the sharp peak,
thin rising left stem, broad right stroke, serif feet, fluid waveform, and the cutout at
the wave crossing. Crisp edges; no texture, shadow, glow, 3D, paper, or checkerboard.
Center the mark with wide clean white space. Exactly one symbol, no extra text.
